Understanding the options

There is more than one way to access support.

SEAI support is available through individual home-energy grants, while some eligible households may qualify for fully funded works through the Warmer Homes Scheme.

Grant approval must normally be in place before work begins, and eligible work must be completed by the appropriate SEAI-registered contractor.

General eligibility

Could your property qualify?

Exact rules vary by measure, but most individual home-energy grant applications involve the following requirements.

  • You are the owner or eligible applicant for a property in Ireland.
  • The property meets the required construction and occupancy date for the selected measure.
  • You apply and receive a grant offer before starting eligible work.
  • You use an SEAI-registered contractor for the relevant measure.
  • The proposed work meets SEAI technical standards and scheme rules.
  • A BER, technical assessment or other supporting documentation is provided where required.

Fully funded upgrades

The Warmer Homes Scheme.

Eligible homeowners may receive fully funded energy upgrades selected following an SEAI assessment. You must meet all scheme conditions.

Own and occupy

The property must be your main home and you must own it.

Home age

The home must have been built and occupied before 2006.

Qualifying payment

You must receive one of the welfare payments listed by SEAI.

Suitable property

An assessment must confirm that appropriate improvements can be completed.
