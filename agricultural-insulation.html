Agricultural Insulation

Spray foam insulation for sheds and containers.

Ecospec provides spray foam insulation for suitable agricultural sheds, farm buildings and storage containers. The system forms a continuous layer across the treated surface, helping to reduce heat loss, control air movement and limit condensation risk.

Why insulate agricultural buildings?

Improve comfort and help protect the inside of the structure.

Metal sheds and containers can heat up quickly in warm weather and lose heat rapidly when temperatures fall.

Changes in temperature can also contribute to condensation forming on cold metal surfaces. Over time, this may affect stored materials, tools, equipment and the internal condition of the structure.

Spray foam follows the shape of the roof and walls, creating a continuous insulation layer across suitable surfaces.

Agricultural buildings Sheds
Spray foam for sheds

A continuous insulation layer for suitable farm buildings.

Spray foam can be applied to suitable metal or framed shed roofs and walls, creating insulation that follows the shape of the building.

It may be used in workshops, machinery sheds, storage buildings and other agricultural spaces where improved thermal performance and condensation control are required.

The roof and wall sheets, structural frame, fixings, moisture conditions and ventilation requirements must be inspected before installation.

  • Helps reduce heat loss through roofs and walls
  • Can help limit condensation on suitable metal surfaces
  • Forms around structural and detailed areas
  • Useful for workshops, stores and machinery sheds
  • Installed after a full building assessment

Spray foam for containers

Insulate steel containers for storage, work or utility use.

Steel containers are highly responsive to outside temperatures, becoming cold in winter and hot in summer.

Spray foam can be applied to suitable internal roof and wall surfaces to form a continuous insulating layer. This can help create a more stable internal environment and reduce condensation on the treated metal.

The container must be dry, structurally sound and properly prepared. Ventilation, intended use, electrical work and any internal lining should be considered before installation.

  • Suitable for selected steel storage containers
  • Applied to prepared internal roof and wall surfaces
  • Helps reduce rapid temperature changes
  • Can help control condensation on treated areas
  • May be covered with an appropriate internal lining

Before installation

The structure must be suitable and properly prepared.

Spray foam should not be used to hide leaks, corrosion, damaged sheeting or unresolved moisture problems. Repairs should be completed before insulation is installed.

The intended use of the shed or container also affects ventilation, fire protection, internal finishes and the type of foam system that may be suitable.

  • Condition of roof and wall sheeting
  • Leaks, corrosion and existing condensation
  • Ventilation and intended building use
  • Electrical services and heat-producing equipment
  • Required internal lining or protective coating
