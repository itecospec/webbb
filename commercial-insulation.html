Why commercial insulation matters

Improve comfort, control heat loss and support building performance.

Commercial buildings often have large roof and wall areas, making insulation an important part of controlling heat loss and maintaining a more stable internal environment.

The right insulation system can help improve occupant comfort, reduce unnecessary energy use and support the overall performance of the building fabric.

Ecospec reviews the building type, existing construction, access, moisture conditions, fire requirements and operating environment before recommending a solution.

Commercial wool insulation

Thermal and acoustic insulation for commercial structures.

Mineral wool can be installed within suitable roof, ceiling, wall and partition systems to reduce heat loss and help control sound.

It is commonly used in offices, warehouses, industrial units and other commercial buildings because it can be fitted around structural elements, service routes and detailed areas.

The product type, density and thickness are selected according to the building construction and the required thermal, acoustic and fire performance.

  • Suitable for many roof, ceiling and wall build-ups
  • Supports thermal and acoustic performance
  • Available in rolls, slabs and specialist products
  • Can be installed around services and structural details
  • Specified to suit the commercial project

Commercial cavity-wall insulation

Improve the performance of suitable masonry cavity walls.

Cavity-wall insulation can help reduce heat loss through large external wall areas without significantly changing the appearance of the building.

Depending on the wall construction and project requirements, the cavity may be suitable for bonded bead, an injected foam system or a mineral-based insulation system.

Before any work begins, the cavity width, existing insulation, masonry condition, exposure, wall ties and signs of moisture must be assessed.

  • Suitable for assessed masonry cavity walls
  • Installed through a controlled drilling pattern
  • Helps reduce heat loss through external walls
  • Can be used in selected refurbishment projects
  • Requires a cavity survey before installation

FIRETITE insulation

A mineral-based cavity system for suitable commercial projects.

FIRETITE is an injected mineral-based insulation system used in suitable masonry cavity walls where thermal performance and the fire classification of the system are important considerations.

The material is installed into the cavity to form a consistent insulation layer. It may be considered for selected commercial, refurbishment and new-build applications.

The wall construction, cavity dimensions and project specification must be reviewed before FIRETITE is selected and installed.

  • Mineral-based injected cavity insulation
  • Suitable for specified commercial applications
  • Forms a full-fill layer inside the cavity
  • Supports thermal performance
  • Installed to the approved system specification

Commercial spray foam

A continuous insulation layer for suitable roofs and structures.

Spray foam can be applied to suitable roof decks, walls and other commercial building elements where a continuous insulation layer is required.

The material expands during installation and follows the shape of the treated surface, making it useful for irregular areas and detailed roof structures.

The substrate, roof condition, moisture, ventilation and fire requirements must be assessed before a spray foam system is specified.

  • Applied directly to suitable building surfaces
  • Forms a continuous insulation layer
  • Useful for detailed or irregular areas
  • Can help reduce unwanted air movement
  • Requires specialist assessment and installation
