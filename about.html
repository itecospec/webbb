Our story

Built around good advice, careful work and lasting results.

Ecospec was founded by TJ Byrne and Jordan Drum with a clear aim: to build a green, sustainable company delivering dependable insulation and ventilation solutions.

Today, that same purpose shapes every project. We combine practical technical knowledge with modern products and professional installation to help improve comfort, reduce heat loss and create healthier buildings.

Every client is valued, every property is treated with care and every recommendation is based on what is genuinely suitable for the building.
